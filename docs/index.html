Page cleanup and image settings

Every page is enlarged to the best size for its letters, contrast-boosted, de-speckled, deskewed, curved lines are straightened, headers, page numbers, catchwords, margin notes and specks are removed (page cleanup, can be turned off), columns are found and read right to left.

Add photos or a PDF to start.

The first page takes a little longer while the models (about 12 MB) load into your browser. After that they are cached.

✓

Text

Check each page next to its picture. You can correct the text right in the box — downloads use your corrections. “What was done” under each page lists every clean-up step and every word that was fixed automatically. Lines like [[רש״י]] mark where each part of the page (Gemara, Rashi, Tosafos…) starts.

    No AI, no API key — runs entirely in your browser with our trained Tesseract models. Your pages never leave your computer; this site loads only its own files.

    Engine: Tesseract.js (Apache 2.0) with our fine-tuned models (Nov 2026, trained on real printed lines from public-domain Vilna and Prague scans; code, models and test numbers at hebrew-ocr-offline): heb_rashi7 for Rashi script, from the heb_rashi model by Zeev Pekar / pninim.org, and heb4 for square letters, from tessdata_best heb (both Apache 2.0), plus pninim.org's word list and a word-frequency list built from Sefaria's open texts. PDF reading by pdf.js (Apache 2.0), PDF writing by pdf-lib (MIT); iPhone HEIC photos by heic2any (MIT).
